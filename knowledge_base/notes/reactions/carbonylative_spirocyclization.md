### Mechanism Overview
Key mechanistic steps, rate-limiting step, why these conditions matter electronically/sterically.
*   The transformation proceeds through a palladium-catalyzed cascade: oxidation of a 1,2-diol to a dicarbonyl by DDQ, nucleophilic addition of a cyclopropanol to the ketone, followed by palladium insertion into the cyclopropanol C-C bond and carbonyl insertion to form the spirolactone. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 582-600; *   The use of DDQ is critical as a stoichiometric oxidant to generate the reactive 1,2-dicarbonyl in situ, which activates the cyclopropanol for ring opening. (Org. *   **Over-reduction or over-addition** during the Grignard steps is a key concern.
