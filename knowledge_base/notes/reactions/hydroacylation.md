### Reaction Type

Hydroacylation of aryl alkenes with aroyl fluorides using a nickel/silane catalyst system.

### Mechanism Overview

Nickel(II) is reduced in situ by triethoxysilane to generate a Ni-H species. This hydride adds across the aryl alkene to form a nickel-alkyl intermediate. Aroyl fluoride oxidative addition, followed by reductive elimination, yields the α-aryl ketone product. The potassium phosphate base likely facilitates Si-H activation and/or acts as a fluoride scavenger. The reaction is catalytic in nickel(II) chloride (0.15 equiv) and proceeds at room temperature (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367;

### Solvent Notes

✓ **Anhydrous DMF** — The solvent is essential for reaction success. It solubilizes the nickel catalyst, base, and substrates, and its polarity likely facilitates the catalytic cycle.

### Reagent and Catalyst Notes

✓ **Nickel(II) chloride ethylene glycol dimethyl ether complex** — This pre-complexed catalyst is more stable to air/moisture and easier to handle than simpler nickel salts (Note 3).
✓ **Triethoxysilane** — Acts as the stoichiometric reductant and hydride source. Must be stored and handled in an inert atmosphere (glovebox) to prevent decomposition and maintain yield (Note 2, 6).
✓ **Potassium phosphate tribasic (K₃PO₄)** — A mild, non-nucleophilic inorganic base. Must be stored in a glovebox to prevent hydration which could impact activity (Note 2, 4) (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367;

### Substrate Scope and Limitations

✓ Works with **terminal aryl alkenes** (e.g., 4-acetoxystyrene) and **internal aryl alkenes** (e.g., (E)-stilbene).
✓ **Aroyl fluorides** serve as effective acyl donors. Benzoyl fluoride was used with 1.5 equiv relative to the alkene.
✗ The scope/limitations regarding other olefin types (e.g., aliphatic alkenes) or other acyl sources (e.g., acid chlorides, anhydrides) are not detailed in this source (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367;

### Functional Group Tolerance

✓ **Tolerates:** Acetate (phenolic acetate ester). The procedure shows survival of the phenolic acetate ester on the styrene derivative.
✗ **Incompatible:** Unknown for other groups (e.g., free alcohols, amines, aldehydes) as not tested in this source (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367;

### Critical Conditions

✓ **Atmosphere:** All catalyst, base, and silane handling must be performed under an inert atmosphere (nitrogen-filled glovebox) to prevent catalyst deactivation and silane decomposition (Note 2).
✓ **Addition Order:** The silane is added to the mixture of nickel catalyst and base in DMF to pre-form the active nickel-hydride species before alkene/acyl fluoride addition (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367; ✓ **Temperature:** Reaction proceeds efficiently at room temperature (21–22 °C) over 24 hours (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367;

### Side Reactions

Potential side reactions are not explicitly discussed. A notable "tar residue" remains in the flask after workup, suggesting possible oligomerization or decomposition pathways under the reaction conditions (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367;

### Procedure Hints

* A visual color change from light blue to dark blue upon addition of triethoxysilane indicates formation of the active nickel-hydride species (Figure 1C).
* The reaction mixture color further changes over time (Figure 2), which can serve as a rough qualitative indicator of progress.
* **Quenching:** Addition of saturated aqueous NaHCO₃ is exothermic. Allow the mixture to cool to room temperature before proceeding with extraction (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367;

### Scale-up Notes

* The **exotherm upon quenching** with saturated NaHCO₃ is noted. On larger scale, this addition must be controlled and cooling may be required.
* During column chromatography, **lower pressure** (from compressed air) was found to improve column performance (Note 17) (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367;

### Analytical Notes

* **TLC:** Product (3) has Rf = 0.25, and starting alkene (1) has Rf = 0.59 in ethyl acetate/hexane (1:5, v/v) (Note 9, Figure 6).
* **Purification:** Product elutes in fractions 34-75 using a detailed gradient elution on silica gel (Note 17, Figure 7).
* **Purity Determination:** Quantitative ¹H NMR using 1,1,2,2-tetrachloroethane as an internal standard is a reliable method (Note 19) (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367;

### Yield / Troubleshooting Tips

* **Yield (62%):** The reported isolated yield is moderate. The formation of tar suggests competing decomposition pathways.
* **Key Yield Factor:** **Strict anhydrous/inert handling of triethoxysilane** is critical. A "notable decrease in yield" was observed when silane stored outside the glovebox was used (Note 6).
* **Workup:** Stirring the quenched mixture for an additional 20 minutes ensures complete destruction of any residual triethoxysilane (Note 11) (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 367;
