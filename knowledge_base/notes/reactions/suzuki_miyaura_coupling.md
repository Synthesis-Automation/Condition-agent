### Reaction Type

Buchwald-Hartwig amination (Pd-catalyzed C–N coupling) and Suzuki-Miyaura cross-coupling (Pd-catalyzed C–C coupling) of aryl mesylates.

### Mechanism Overview

* Aryl mesylates act as electrophiles via oxidative addition to a Pd(0)-CM-phos complex, where the electron-rich, bulky phosphine ligand facilitates addition to the challenging C(aryl)–OMs bond (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* For amination, the rate-limiting step is typically reductive elimination from the Pd(II)-amido complex; the ligand accelerates this step (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* For Suzuki coupling, transmetalation with the arylboronic acid is critical; the phosphate base (K₃PO₄) activates the boronic acid while the ligand stabilizes the Pd intermediate (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).

### Solvent Notes

* **tert-Butyl alcohol** is preferred for both couplings with aryl mesylates — it is a high-boiling, polar protic solvent that dissolves inorganic bases and is compatible with the Pd/CM-phos catalyst system at elevated temperatures (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Dichloromethane** is used as a solvent for pre-forming the active Pd catalyst from Pd(OAc)₂ and CM-phos with Et₃N as a base, prior to solvent removal and addition of coupling partners (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).

### Reagent and Catalyst Notes

* **CM-phos ligand** is critical for success with aryl mesylates, as less electron-rich/bulky ligands may fail to promote oxidative addition (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Pre-forming the catalyst** by stirring Pd(OAc)₂ and CM-phos in CH₂Cl₂/Et₃N at 50°C for 5 minutes generates the active Pd(0) species before adding substrates, improving reproducibility (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Phenylboronic acid** is used in excess (2.0 equiv for Suzuki) to drive the reaction to completion and should be recrystallized prior to use to ensure quality (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Potassium phosphate (K₃PO₄)** is the base for Suzuki coupling, while **potassium carbonate (K₂CO₃)** is used for the Buchwald-Hartwig amination (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).

### Substrate Scope and Limitations

* **Aryl mesylates** derived from phenols are effective electrophiles, with electron-donating groups (e.g., *tert*-butyl) ortho to the OMs group being suitable (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Nucleophiles**: Secondary amines (e.g., N-methylaniline) work well for C–N coupling. Arylboronic acids (e.g., phenylboronic acid) work for C–C coupling (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Limitations**: The procedure may not be general for highly electron-deficient aryl mesylates or sterically hindered nucleophiles without further optimization (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).

### Functional Group Tolerance

* **Tolerates**: Alkyl groups (e.g., *tert*-butyl), aryl rings (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Incompatible**: Not explicitly tested, but conditions (strong base, Pd catalyst) are likely incompatible with base-sensitive or reducible groups (e.g., aldehydes, alkyl halides) (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).

### Critical Conditions

* **Temperature**: Reactions are run at **120°C** in a sealed vessel; this high temperature is necessary for reactivity with the robust aryl OMs bond on large scale (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Atmosphere**: The catalyst is pre-formed under **nitrogen**, and coupling reactions are run in a sealed Schlenk flask to exclude air/moisture (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Reaction Time**: **24 hours** is required for complete conversion on the reported scale; shorter times may give lower yields (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).

### Side Reactions

* **Homocoupling of the boronic acid** is a potential side reaction in Suzuki couplings; using purified boronic acid and ensuring an inert atmosphere helps suppress this (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Hydrolysis of the mesylate** could compete, but is minimized by using anhydrous conditions and non-aqueous solvent (*t*-BuOH) (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Decomposition of the catalyst or ligand** at high temperature over long periods is possible, emphasizing the need for a well-defined pre-catalyst formation step (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).

### Procedure Hints

* **Efficient stirring is critical** at large scale to prevent the formation of a sticky, glutinous mixture that lowers yield (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Use a blast shield** when heating *t*-BuOH (bp 82°C) in a sealed vessel at 120°C due to increased pressure (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).

### Scale-up Notes

* **Internal temperature** is lower in large-scale reactions, requiring a **higher oil bath temperature (120°C)** than might be used on small scale to achieve the same effective reaction temperature (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Reaction times are longer** on large scale (24 h) compared to smaller-scale optimizations (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Mixing** becomes paramount; use an appropriately large stir bar to avoid inefficient stirring and heterogeneity (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).

### Analytical Notes

* **TLC for Mesylate Formation**: Rf values in ethyl acetate/dichloromethane/hexane (1:2:7): SM (phenol) = 0.43, product (mesylate) = 0.50 (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **¹H NMR for Product Identification**: Key diagnostic signals are provided for the mesylate, amination product, and biphenyl product (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).

### Yield / Troubleshooting Tips

* **Yields are high (79-92%)** when the procedure is followed precisely, including catalyst pre-formation, strict inert atmosphere, and efficient stirring (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Low yields** may result from: 1) inefficient stirring leading to poor mixing, 2) inferior quality or decomposed boronic acid, 3) insufficient reaction time or temperature, or 4) incomplete catalyst pre-activation (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
* **Purification** for all compounds involves column chromatography on silica gel with hexane/ethyl acetate or hexane/dichloromethane gradients (Org. Synth. 2015, 92, 195-212; prep v92p0195).
