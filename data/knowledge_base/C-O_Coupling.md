# Reaction Family: CO_Coupling - C-O Coupling

## Summary

- C-O bond formation (aryl etherification) from aryl/heteroaryl halides and alcohols/phenols.

## Applicability

- Substrates: aryl/heteroaryl halides with alcohols or phenols.
- Functional groups tolerated: depends on Pd vs Cu systems and ligand choice.
- Common incompatibilities: sterically hindered alcohols and some heterocycles.
