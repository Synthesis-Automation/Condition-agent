# Buchwald Hartwig Amination — Reaction Notes

### Reaction Type
Palladium/biarylphosphine-catalyzed Buchwald–Hartwig amination of aryl mesylates with amines

### Solvent Notes
• Good: CH2Cl2 — anhydrous dichloromethane dissolves Pd(OAc)2 and ligand for rapid precatalyst formation (Org. Synth.)
• Good: tBuOH — protic high-bp solvent dissolves inorganic bases (K2CO3, K3PO4); use blast shield above 82 °C due to pressure buildup (Org. Synth.)
• Et3N — dual role as base and co-solvent for catalyst activation; ensure dryness to prevent catalyst deactivation (Org. Synth.)

### Reagent and Catalyst Notes
• Pre-activate Pd(OAc)2 (1 mol%) with biaryl phosphine (CM-Phos, 4 mol%) in CH2Cl2/Et3N at 50 °C for 5 min, then strip volatiles to isolate active precatalyst (Org. Synth.)
• K2CO3 for Buchwald–Hartwig aminations—readily available and effective with tBuOH (Org. Synth.)
• Dry and degas all solvents and reagents to avoid catalyst poisoning (Org. Synth.)
• Use less active ligands (e.g., Xantphos) to improve selectivity when over-coupling occurs (Org. Synth.)

### Side Reactions
• Poor stirring on scale leads to sticky, glutinous mixtures and lower yields—ensure vigorous agitation (Org. Synth.)
• Sealed-vessel heating of tBuOH above its bp can cause dangerous overpressure—use blast shield (Org. Synth.)

### Substrate Scope and Limitations
• Aryl mesylates bearing electron-neutral or weakly donating groups couple efficiently under Pd/CM-Phos conditions (Org. Synth.)
• Large-scale reactions require longer times (24 h) than small-scale; sterically hindered aryl sulfonates may need higher ligand loading (Org. Synth.)

### Critical Conditions
• Rigorously exclude air and moisture: evacuate/backfill flask three times with N2 before catalyst activation (Org. Synth.)
• After precatalyst formation, strip volatiles at high vacuum (0.01 mmHg) to obtain solid pre-catalyst, then add substrate/base/solvent (Org. Synth.)
• Pre-stir at room temperature 10 min before ramping to 120 °C for 24 h (Org. Synth.)

### Yield / Troubleshooting Tips
• Crystallization of aryl mesylate from CH2Cl2/hexane at –14 °C yields analytically pure material and simplifies workup (Org. Synth.)
• Dry crude product under high vacuum after evaporation to remove residual tBuOH or Et3N (Org. Synth.)
