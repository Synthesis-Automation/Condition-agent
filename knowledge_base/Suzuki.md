# Reaction Family: Suzuki_CC - Suzuki-Miyaura Coupling

## Summary

- Pd-catalyzed cross-coupling of aryl/heteroaryl halides with boronates to form C-C bonds.

## Applicability

- Substrates: aryl and heteroaryl halides; aryl and heteroaryl boronates.
- Functional groups tolerated: broad, but heterocycles often need tailored catalysts.
- Common incompatibilities: base/solvent sensitivity; some substrates need ligand-tuned systems.
