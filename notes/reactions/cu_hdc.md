### Reaction Type
Copper-catalyzed coupling of primary alkyl iodides with aryl boronic acids.


### Mechanism Overview
*   Key step is a copper(I)/copper(III) catalytic cycle, where transmetalation from boron to copper precedes oxidative addition of the alkyl iodide. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   The β-hydride elimination pathway, a major side reaction for alkyl electrophiles, is suppressed by using primary alkyl iodides and a bidentate ligand (TMEDA). (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Sodium tert-butoxide is believed to activate the boronic acid via formation of a boronate species. (Org. Synth. 2025)

### Solvent Notes
*   ✓ Good: Toluene — Aromatic, non-polar solvents like toluene are optimal for Cu/TMEDA catalysis, providing a balance of solubility for reagents and suppressing undesired pathways. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   ✗ Avoid: THF, DMF, DMSO — Polar aprotic solvents lead to significant side-product formation (e.g., reduction of the alkyl iodide) in this copper-catalyzed system. (Org. Synth. 2025)

### Reagent and Catalyst Notes
*   Catalyst system is composed of CuI and the inexpensive, air-stable bidentate ligand TMEDA (1,2 equiv per Cu). Pre-mixing CuI and TMEDA before adding other reagents is critical for activity. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Primary alkyl iodides are required; bromides are unreactive under these conditions. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Sodium tert-butoxide is the required base; other alkoxide bases (KOtBu, LiOtBu) give significantly lower yields. (Org. Synth. 2025)

### Substrate Scope and Limitations
*   Works well for coupling primary alkyl iodides (linear and β-branched) with electron-neutral and electron-rich aryl boronic acids. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Electron-deficient aryl boronic acids give poor yields. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Secondary alkyl iodides are not viable substrates due to competitive β-hydride elimination. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   The reaction is limited to aryl boronic acids; alkyl boronic acids are not reported. (Org. Synth. 2025)

### Functional Group Tolerance
*   ✓ Tolerates: Silyl ethers (TBDPS), alkyl chlorides, esters, nitriles, aromatic halides (Br, I). (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   ✗ Incompatible: Free alcohols, primary amines, aldehydes, ketones — These protic or strongly coordinating groups are not tolerated, likely due to interference with the copper catalyst. (Org. Synth. 2025)

### Critical Conditions
*   Reaction requires anhydrous, inert (N₂ or Ar) atmosphere. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Temperature is critical: optimal at 80 °C. Lower temperatures (60 °C) slow the reaction; higher temperatures (110 °C) increase side reactions. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Order of addition is key: CuI and TMEDA must be mixed first in toluene, followed by NaOtBu, boronic acid, and finally the alkyl iodide. Adding the alkyl iodide last minimizes homocoupling. (Org. Synth. 2025)

### Side Reactions
*   **Homocoupling of the alkyl iodide** (reduction to alkane): Minimized by strict adherence to the specified order of addition. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   **β-Hydride elimination**: Leads to alkene byproducts. Suppressed by using primary alkyl iodides and the TMEDA ligand. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   **Protodeboronation**: Observed as a minor side reaction. (Org. Synth. 2025)

### Procedure Hints
*   Perform all operations under an inert atmosphere. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Pre-mix CuI and TMEDA in toluene for 5 minutes before adding other reagents. The solution turns from colorless to bright yellow/orange. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Add the alkyl iodide last, dropwise via syringe. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Reaction progress can be monitored by the formation of a black precipitate (presumably Cu⁰). (Org. Synth. 2025)

### Scale-up Notes
*   The procedure has been demonstrated on a 10 mmol scale. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   On scale, ensure efficient stirring to maintain a homogeneous mixture, especially after the black precipitate forms. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   The exotherm is mild, but controlled addition of the alkyl iodide is still recommended. (Org. Synth. 2025)

### Analytical Notes
*   Reaction progress is easily monitored by TLC (visualized with KMnO₄). The alkyl iodide starting material has a low Rf; the product has a higher Rf. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   A diagnostic for completion is the formation of a fine black precipitate (metallic copper). (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   The product arylalkane is typically less polar than the boronic acid starting material. (Org. Synth. 2025)

### Yield / Troubleshooting Tips
*   Low yields often stem from incorrect order of addition or the presence of air/moisture. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   If homocoupling (alkane formation) is dominant, verify that the alkyl iodide is being added last to the pre-formed catalytic mixture. (Org. Synth. 2025)
*   Purification is straightforward by silica gel chromatography, as the product is typically the least polar major component. (Org. Synth. 2025)
