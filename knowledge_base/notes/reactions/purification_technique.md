### Procedure Hints
* Flash column chromatography can be effectively performed using either isocratic or gradient elution protocols to achieve high purity and recovery. (Danheiser, Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 276-302; * Both "wet loading" (dissolving sample in eluent) and "dry-loading" (adsorbing onto silica or Celite) are viable methods for applying the sample to the column. (Danheiser, Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 276-302)
* The use of different grades of silica gel and different column packing methods can be successfully applied, indicating the technique is robust across common variations. (Danheiser, Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 276-302)
