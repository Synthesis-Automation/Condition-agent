# Radical Borylation — Reaction Notes

### Reaction Type
Transition-metal-free radical borylation of alkyl halides using B₂pin₂ and t-BuOLi as radical initiator

### Solvent Notes
• MeOH/H₂O mixture is tolerated; trace water does not impair conversion (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)
• Use high-purity methanol to avoid impurities, but rigorous drying is unnecessary (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)

### Reagent and Catalyst Notes
• t-BuOLi serves as both base and radical initiator; add under inert atmosphere and avoid dusty handling (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)
• Employ excess B₂pin₂ (≥3 equiv small scale; 4 equiv large scale) to outcompete solvent-mediated radical reduction (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)

### Side Reactions
• Alkane formation via alkyl radical reduction by MeOH increases if B₂pin₂ is substoichiometric; suppress by raising [B₂pin₂] (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)

### Substrate Scope and Limitations
• Primary and secondary alkyl iodides (and likely bromides) are efficiently borylated; a variety of functional groups are tolerated (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)
• Large-scale runs develop a solid phase that can cause nonuniform mixing; remedy by increasing reagent concentrations and thorough stirring (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)

### Critical Conditions
• Premix alkyl halide in MeOH/H₂O before base addition to ensure homogeneous initiation (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)
• Perform multiple vacuum/N₂ backfill cycles to exclude O₂ and prevent radical termination (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)
• Maintain 50 °C for extended periods (≈56 h); lower temperature or shorter reaction times lead to incomplete conversion (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)

### Yield / Troubleshooting Tips
• Removing excess B₂pin₂ by low-temperature precipitation/filtration can cofiltrate product; monitor precipitation and minimize handling loss (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)
• Half-scale reactions mirror yields (~70%); for 50 g scale, adjust base and B₂pin₂ loadings upward to retain conversion (Liu et al., Org. Synth. 2022)
