### Reaction Type
Two-step oxidative nitrogen-atom insertion into cyclopentenones via silyl enol ether formation followed by PIDA-mediated amination.

### Mechanism Overview
*   The reaction proceeds via formation of a silyl enol ether, which reacts with ammonium carbamate as a nitrogen source in the presence of a hypervalent iodine oxidant (PIDA) to effect a formal two-electron oxidation and C–N bond formation, yielding a 2-pyridone core. The silyl group activates the enone toward amination and controls regioselectivity. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476; 
### Solvent Notes
*   THF is used for the initial silyl enol ether formation due to its ability to dissolve reactants and stabilize the intermediate. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   Methanol is added after enol ether formation to provide a protic medium for the subsequent oxidative amination step with ammonium carbamate and PIDA. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   DCM is suitable for the workup and extraction due to its good solubility for the product and immiscibility with aqueous sodium bicarbonate. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*

### Reagent and Catalyst Notes
*   **TBSOTf** is a highly corrosive and moisture-sensitive silylating agent; fumes are observed upon addition to the reaction mixture. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   **Ammonium carbamate** serves as a convenient, bench-stable source of ammonia for the amination. Using less than 4 equivalents leads to lower yields. Larger lumps do not adversely affect the reaction outcome. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   **(Diacetoxyiodo)benzene (PIDA)** is the stoichiometric oxidant. While stoichiometry requires 2 equiv, using 3 equiv (50% excess) is optimal for achieving high yields. The reaction mixture turns yellow upon its addition. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   **Triethylamine** is used as a base to scavenge the triflic acid generated during silyl enol ether formation. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*

### Substrate Scope and Limitations
*   Demonstrated on a 6-methoxy-substituted 2,3-dihydro-1H-inden-1-one (a benzo-fused cyclopentenone) to give a 7-methoxyisoquinolin-1(2H)-one. The method is general for oxidative nitrogen atom insertion into cyclopentenones. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*

### Functional Group Tolerance
*   ✓ **Tolerates:** Aryl methyl ether (methoxy group) is stable under the reaction and workup conditions. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   The oxidative conditions using PIDA and ammonium carbamate may not be compatible with strongly reducing or oxidizable functional groups (e.g., thiols, some amines, aldehydes). The scope discussion implies this limitation. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*

### Critical Conditions
*   **Order of addition** is critical: TBSOTf must be added last to the mixture of substrate, THF, and Et₃N to ensure efficient silyl enol ether formation. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   The reaction is performed **in air** at **room temperature**. No inert atmosphere is required. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   The oxidative amination step (after adding MeOH, ammonium carbamate, and PIDA) is complete within **30 minutes** at room temperature. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*

### Side Reactions
*   The primary side reaction appears to be over-oxidation or the formation of minor, closely eluting impurities during chromatography. These can be avoided by careful fraction collection. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*

### Procedure Hints
*   Full procedure available in source file.
*   After adding PIDA, the reaction mixture turns yellow, providing a visual indicator that the oxidation step has commenced. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   The first filtration after the 30-minute reaction time is primarily to remove undissolved ammonium carbamate lumps and can be omitted if finely ground material is used. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   Rotary evaporation of the THF/MeOH mixture can be done at 40°C without safety concerns, as the oxidant is fully consumed. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*

### Scale-up Notes
*   A 15 mmol scale run (half of the reported scale) proceeded successfully with a simplified column gradient (DCM to 98:2 DCM/MeOH) and required only one chromatographic purification. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   At scale, efficient mixing is important to ensure homogeneity, especially after adding solid ammonium carbamate. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*

### Analytical Notes
*   **TLC Conditions:** SiO₂, 95:5 DCM/MeOH. Starting material Rf = 0.83, product Rf = 0.28. Visualize with KMnO₄ stain. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   **Purity Determination:** Quantitative ¹H NMR (qNMR) using 1,3,5-trimethoxybenzene as an internal standard in d6-DMSO is an effective method for determining product purity without relying on chromatography standards. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*

### Yield / Troubleshooting Tips
*   The reported yield is 68% on 30 mmol scale. A 15 mmol scale run gave a slightly lower yield of 62%. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   For purification, a slow, stepwise gradient (e.g., 100:0 to 95:5 DCM/MeOH in small increments) is necessary to separate the product from closely eluting impurities. Collecting only the pure middle fractions and re-purifying the early fractions is an effective strategy. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
*   The product can be further purified by recrystallization from DCM (dissolving in hot DCM with stirring, then cooling to room temperature, 10°C, and finally -30°C) to give white crystals. *(Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 465-476)*
