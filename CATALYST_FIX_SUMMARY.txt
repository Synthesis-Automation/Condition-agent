KEY INSIGHTS
================================================================================

1. 44% of C-N coupling reactions are NON-CATALYZED
   - SNAr (nucleophilic aromatic substitution)
   - Direct nucleophilic substitution
   - Base-promoted alkylation

2. Bases like K2CO3, DIPEA, Et3N are REAGENTS
   - Used in 1-3 equivalents (stoichiometric)
   - Not regenerated (consumed in reaction)
   - Role: deprotonation, acid scavenging

3. True organocatalysts would be:
   - Proline (<20 mol%)
   - DMAP (<20 mol%)
   - DBU (<20 mol%)
   - Thioureas, NHCs
   - None found in this dataset

4. Catalyst filtering still works perfectly:

   - Filter to "other" for non-catalyzed
