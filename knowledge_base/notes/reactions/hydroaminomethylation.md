### Reaction Type

Hydroaminomethylation (metal-free, acid-mediated).

### Mechanism Overview

* The reaction proceeds via in-situ generation of an iminium ion from an aminal intermediate; this electrophile is intercepted by an unactivated alkene in a hydroamination-type addition. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 414-427;

### Solvent Notes

* No solvent is used for the key C–C bond-forming step; the reaction is run neat in a mixture of the aminal reagent and trifluoroacetic acid. This high concentration is likely critical for the intermolecular reaction. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 414-427;

### Reagent and Catalyst Notes

* The tetrabenzylmethanediamine aminal serves as a stable, solid precursor to a bis-iminium ion, releasing two equivalents of dibenzylamine in situ. Using the pre-formed aminal prevents handling gaseous formaldehyde and simplifies stoichiometry. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 414-427; *   Trifluoroacetic acid is used stoichiometrically (0.6 M relative to alkene) to protonate the aminal and generate the reactive electrophile. Its strength is sufficient for activation but mild enough to avoid side reactions like polymerization of the alkene. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 414-427;

### Substrate Scope and Limitations

* The method is effective for unactivated, terminal aliphatic alkenes, providing linear (anti-Markovnikov) amine products selectively. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 414-427; *   The original publication notes that internal, cyclic, and conjugated alkenes are not suitable substrates for this specific acid-mediated variant. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 414-427;

### Critical Conditions

* Temperature control is crucial: the aminal is pre-formed at 0°C, and the TFA is added at 0°C before warming to 75°C for the C–C coupling. This staged protocol prevents premature decomposition. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 414-427; *   The reaction mixture transitions from a suspension to a clear orange solution upon heating; this visual change is a key indicator of reaction initiation. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 414-427;

### Side Reactions

* Potential oligomerization or polymerization of the alkene could occur under the strongly acidic conditions, but the use of TFA and specific temperature protocol minimizes this. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 414-427; *   Hydrolysis of the iminium species or the aminal could lead to formaldehyde/amine by-products. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 414-427;
