### Reaction Type
Key reaction: Amino-hydroxylation via tethered nitrenium ion formation.

### Mechanism Overview
*   An allylic methoxycarbamate substrate is activated by a hypervalent iodine reagent (PIFA) to generate a tethered nitrenium ion. This nitrenium ion undergoes intramolecular anti-addition to the alkene, forming an oxazolidinone ring and creating a new C–O and C–N bond with diastereoselectivity controlled by the tether. The rate-limiting step is the formation of the nitrenium ion by ligand exchange on iodine(III). (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511; 
### Solvent Notes
*   **CH₂Cl₂** — Preferred solvent for the nitrenium ion-mediated cyclization. Its moderate polarity effectively solubilizes both the organic substrate and the polar hypervalent iodine reagent (PIFA) and is inert under the oxidative conditions. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **Pyridine** — Used as both a solvent and base for nucleophilic substitution with methoxyamine. Its basicity deprotonates methoxyamine hydrochloride, generating the free nucleophile in situ. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)

### Reagent and Catalyst Notes
*   **[Bis(trifluoroacetoxy)iodo]benzene (PIFA)** — The hypervalent iodine reagent of choice for generating nitrenium ions from carbamates. The trifluoroacetoxy ligands are good leaving groups, facilitating the key ligand exchange step. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **1,1'-Carbonyldiimidazole (CDI)** — A safe and efficient coupling reagent for converting alcohols to imidazolyl carbamates, which are superior activated intermediates for nucleophilic substitution with amines compared to traditional acyl chlorides or anhydrides, as they avoid strong acid/base conditions. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **Methoxyamine hydrochloride** — The nitrogen source. The methoxy group serves as a protecting group for the amine in the final product and is part of the tether directing the cyclization. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)

### Substrate Scope and Limitations
*   **Works for:** (E)-disubstituted alkenes. The procedure demonstrates scalability for simple allylic alcohols. The tether (methoxycarbamate) is essential for the intramolecular cyclization. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **Limitations/Unknown:** Scope for internal alkene geometry (Z-isomers), trisubstituted alkenes, or substrates with sterically hindered allylic positions is not demonstrated in this specific procedure. The diastereoselectivity outcome is likely substrate-dependent. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)

### Functional Group Tolerance
*   ✓ **Tolerates:** Alkene, ester (formed in situ), and the oxazolidinone product under the reaction conditions.
*   ✗ **Incompatible:** The procedure does not demonstrate tolerance for strongly reducing or oxidizing functional groups, or those that might react with PIFA (e.g., phenols, thioethers). Free amines would compete with the desired intramolecular pathway. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)

### Critical Conditions
*   **Stoichiometry:** Using 1.4 equivalents of PIFA is critical for complete conversion of the carbamate starting material. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **Concentration:** The cyclization step (Part B) is run at a relatively low concentration (~0.05 M) in CH₂Cl₂, which may help manage any exotherm and ensure homogeneity. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **Temperature:** The cyclization proceeds at ambient temperature (~24 °C). The subsequent trifluoroacetate deprotection (Part C) is initiated at 0 °C to control the exotherm of ammonia addition before warming to room temperature. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)

### Side Reactions
*   **Iodobenzene formation** — A stoichiometric byproduct of PIFA reduction, which is easily separated during workup and chromatography. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **Over-oxidation** — Not observed under the specified conditions but is a potential risk with hypervalent iodine reagents if reaction times or temperatures are excessively increased. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)

### Procedure Hints
*   The CDI-mediated carbamate formation is complete after 4 hours at room temperature, monitored by NMR. Full procedure available in source file.
*   The PIFA-mediated cyclization develops a yellow color as it proceeds over 4 hours. Full procedure available in source file.
*   **Quenching:** The reaction mixture must be quenched with aqueous sodium thiosulfate to reduce any residual iodine(III) species. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **Atmosphere:** No inert atmosphere (N₂/Ar) is required for any step, indicating the intermediates are not air- or moisture-sensitive under these conditions. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)

### Scale-up Notes
*   The procedure as written is already a large-scale demonstration (~10 mmol scale for the key step). Key scale-up considerations would be managing the exotherm of ammonia addition in the final step and ensuring efficient solvent removal after the PIFA reaction due to the large volume of CH₂Cl₂ used. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)

### Analytical Notes
*   **TLC (Part A):** Product **1** co-elutes with starting alcohol (Rf ~0.5 in 33% EtOAc/hexanes), so NMR is necessary to confirm conversion of the imidazolide intermediate. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **TLC (Part B):** Product **2** (Rf = 0.32) is slightly less polar than starting material **1** (Rf = 0.4) in 33% EtOAc/hexanes. Visualization with KMnO₄ stain is effective. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **TLC (Part C):** Product **3** (Rf = 0.30) is significantly less polar than starting material **2** (Rf = 0.65) in 50% EtOAc/hexanes. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **NMR:** ¹H NMR is sufficient to confirm clean conversion after the CDI step (formation of imidazolide) and after the ammonia deprotection step. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)

### Yield / Troubleshooting Tips
*   **Azeotrope to remove pyridine:** After the methoxyamine substitution, adding heptane and concentrating aids in removing residual pyridine, simplifying later purification. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **Double chromatography:** The cyclized product **2** required two passes through silica gel to separate it completely from iodobenzene, indicating challenging purification. Plan for this when scaling. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
*   **Final product volatility:** Product **3** is a viscous syrup, and complete removal of ethyl acetate (from chromatography) required prolonged high vacuum (18 h). Using a warm water bath (35 °C) can accelerate this process. (Org. Synth. 2025, 102, 494-511)
