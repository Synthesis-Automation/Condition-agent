### Solvent Notes

✓ DMAc/H₂O mixture — high polarity promotes solubilization of K₄[Fe(CN)₆] and aryl halide; water addition must be controlled to maintain homogeneity (Org. Synth. 2020)  
✓ Pre-degassed water — bubbling with inert gas for ≥1 h prevents O₂-induced catalyst deactivation and cyanide oxidation (Org. Synth. 2020)  
✗ Neat DMF or highly protic media — may accelerate ligand hydrolysis or catalyst decomposition at elevated temperatures (implied by need for DMAc/H₂O cosolvent) (Org. Synth. 2020)

### Reagent and Catalyst Notes

• Xantphos with [PdCl(C₃H₅)]₂ at 0.5 mol% delivers high turnover for heteroaryl iodides; bulky bidentate ligand stabilizes low‐valent Pd and suppresses Pd black formation (Org. Synth. 2020)  
• Potassium ferrocyanide is a non‐toxic CN source that slowly releases cyanide under heating; half‐equivalent loading avoids excess free CN⁻ while ensuring complete conversion (Org. Synth. 2020)  
• Argon atmosphere and continuous bubbling (≥300 mL/min) are critical to maintain catalyst activity and prevent oxidative side reactions (Org. Synth. 2020)

### Side Reactions

• Incomplete cyanation and recovery of starting aryl iodide occur if inert gas flow is insufficient or omitted; yields drop from 96% to 3% without argon (Org. Synth. 2020)  
• Slow addition of water avoids localized high concentrations that can precipitate ferrocyanide or quench catalyst activity (Org. Synth. 2020)

### Substrate Scope and Limitations

• Heteroaryl iodides (e.g., indazoles) are well‐tolerated using ferrocyanide/Xantphos; electron-deficient rings benefit from the mild cyanide release (Org. Synth. 2020)  
• Less reactive aryl bromides or chlorides may require higher catalyst loadings or more donating ligands to achieve similar conversions (inferred from general ferrocyanide cyanation literature) (Org. Synth. 2020)

### Critical Conditions

• Degas all solvents and reagents (DMAc, water) by argon bubbling for ≥1 h to exclude O₂ (Org. Synth. 2020)  
• Heat stepwise: first to 60 °C after water addition, then to 95 °C upon catalyst addition to optimize CN transfer and minimize ligand degradation (Org. Synth. 2020)  
• Maintain argon flow throughout (initial 150 mL/min, then ramp to 300 mL/min during cyanation) to ensure anoxic conditions (Org. Synth. 2020)  
• Add water slowly (over 4–5 min) via pressure-equalizing funnel to prevent catalyst quenching and slurry formation (Org. Synth. 2020)

### Yield / Troubleshooting Tips

• Monitor reaction by TLC (hexane/EtOAc 2:1) to distinguish product (Rf ~0.46) and starting material (Rf ~0.32); fluorescence detection at 254 nm is effective (Org. Synth. 2020)  
• If low conversion is observed, verify ligand integrity and increase water degassing time or argon flow to restore catalyst performance (Org. Synth. 2020)  
• Crystallization by slow water addition to the DMAc solution affords high‐purity nitrile and simplifies isolation without extensive chromatography (Org. Synth. 2020)
